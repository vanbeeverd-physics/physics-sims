How big is a joule?

The joule (J) is the unit for energy. Every form of energy can be measured in joules, which is what lets us compare the chemical energy in a banana with the light from a bulb.

One joule is small. It is about the energy it takes to lift a small apple one meter.

1 JLifting a small apple (about 100 g) up one meter
4.18 JWarming 1 gram of water by 1 °C
10 JAn LED bulb shining for one second
4,184 JWarming 1 kilogram of water by 1 °C
about 50,000 JA fully charged phone battery
about 440,000 JThe chemical energy in one banana
about 8,400,000 JThe food a teenager eats in a day
about 120,000,000 JOne gallon of gasoline

The unit is named for James Prescott Joule. In the 1840s he let a falling weight spin a paddle wheel inside a tank of water, then measured the water warming up. His experiment showed that motion and heat are both forms of energy.

Joules, calories and Calories

Food labels use a different energy unit. Watch the capital letter: a food Calorie is 1,000 times bigger than a calorie.

1 Jjoule. The standard unit of energy in science.
1 calcalorie = 4.184 J. Warms 1 g of water by 1 °C.
1 Calfood Calorie = 1,000 cal = 4,184 J. Warms 1 kg of water by 1 °C.
