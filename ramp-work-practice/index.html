    Challenges

    1 · Push it

    On a level wood floor, push the crate. Which force does positive work? Which does negative work? Which forces do zero work, and why?

    2 · Remove friction

    Switch to Ice and push again. What happens to friction’s work? What happens to the speed while you keep pushing?

    3 · Constant speed

    On wood, get the crate moving, then find the applied force that keeps its speed steady. What is the net work? What is ΔKE?

    4 · Tilt the ramp

    Set the angle to 20° and push the crate up. Which force now does negative work? Where does that energy go? (Check the Energy tab.)

    5 · Area = work

    Zero the meters. Push with 100 N for a while, then 50 N. Use the F–x graph areas to calculate the applied work, then check the bar.

    6 · Work–energy theorem

    Try any combination of settings. Is net work always equal to ΔKE? Explain why using the work–energy theorem.
